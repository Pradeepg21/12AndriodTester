## Full description (max 4000)

**This is not community service. This is not a tester swap.**
Twelve Testers gives you 12 dedicated testers for your closed test, so your app is ready to apply for production in 14–15 days — no begging in groups, no testing 20 other apps in return, no testers quietly dropping out on day 9.

New personal developer accounts need at least 12 testers opted in to a closed test for 14 days in a row before they can apply for production access. Finding those testers is the hardest part of launching your first app. We take care of it for you, end to end.

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Submit your app — app name, package name and your closed-testing opt-in link.
2. Pay securely with UPI and upload your payment screenshot.
3. We verify your app details and send you 12 tester email IDs on WhatsApp.
4. Add the 12 emails to your closed test and tap "Emails added".
5. Testers opt in and the 14-day clock starts — track it live, Day X of 14.
6. Get your final report and apply for production.

**WHAT YOU GET**
• 12 committed testers who stay opted in for the full 14 days
• A live progress tracker with every step, from payment to completion
• Two-way chat for each request — reply within 4 hours
• Push notifications whenever your request moves forward
• A final PDF report: testing summary, bugs and crashes found, UX suggestions and a production-readiness checklist
• Multiple apps at once, each tracked separately in New, In Progress and Completed
• Clear refund policy — full refund available until testing starts

**BUILT FOR INDIE DEVELOPERS**
Whether it's your first app or your tenth, you shouldn't lose weeks hunting for testers. Submit in two minutes, then get back to building while we run your closed test.

**TRANSPARENT AND SECURE**
• Sign in with Google or email
• Every step is recorded with a timestamp you can see
• Your data is only visible to you and our support team
• Delete your account anytime from Profile
