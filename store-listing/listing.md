## Full description (max 4000)

**Real testing. Real feedback. Not a tester swap.**
Twelve Testers runs a genuine 14-day closed test of your app with 12 dedicated testers. They install your app from your closed test, use it on their own devices and report the bugs, crashes and usability issues they find — so you can fix them before you launch. No begging in groups, no testing 20 other apps in return.

New personal developer accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before they can apply for production access. That test is your chance to find problems before real users do — we make sure it's done properly, with real people actually using your app.

**HOW IT WORKS**
1. Submit your app — app name, package name and your closed-testing opt-in link.
2. Pay securely with UPI and upload your payment screenshot.
3. We verify your app details and send you 12 tester email IDs on WhatsApp.
4. Add the 12 emails to your closed test and tap "Emails added".
5. Testers opt in and start using your app — track the test live, Day X of 14.
6. Get your final report with everything they found and recommended fixes.

**WHAT YOU GET**
• 12 real testers who use your app on real devices throughout the 14-day test
• Real feedback: bugs, crashes and usability issues found during testing
• A live progress tracker with every step, from payment to completion
• Two-way chat for each request — reply within 4 hours
• Push notifications whenever your request moves forward
• A final PDF report: testing summary, bugs and crashes found, UX suggestions and a production-readiness checklist
• Multiple apps at once, each tracked separately in New, In Progress and Completed
• Clear refund policy — full refund available until testing starts

**BUILT FOR INDIE DEVELOPERS**
Whether it's your first app or your tenth, you shouldn't lose weeks hunting for testers. Submit in two minutes, then get back to building while real testers put your app through its paces.

**TRANSPARENT AND SECURE**
• Sign in with Google or email
• Every step is recorded with a timestamp you can see
• Your data is only visible to you and our support team
• Delete your account anytime from Profile

Questions? Write to supportandroidtester@gmail.com
