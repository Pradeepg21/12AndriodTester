Privacy Policy

Twelve Testers (Android app, package com.satya.a12andriodtester)
Effective date: 11 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Twelve Testers app (“the app”, “we”, “us”) collects, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the choices you have. Twelve Testers is a closed-testing assistance service for app developers: you submit your app, pay the service fee, and our testers run a 14-day closed test and give you feedback and a final report.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2. Permissions

  • Internet — to connect to our servers.
  • Notifications — to tell you when your request changes or you get a reply. You can turn this off in Android settings.
  • Photo picker — only when you choose to upload a payment screenshot. The app sees only the image you select.

3. How we share data

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising. We share it only as follows:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Analytics) stores and processes data on our behalf. Data may be stored on Google servers outside India. See Firebase privacy.
  • Our testers receive your app name and closed-testing opt-in link so they can join your test and use your app.
  • WhatsApp — we send the tester email IDs to the WhatsApp number you provide.
  • Email — activity notifications containing your request details are sent to our support mailbox (Gmail) so our team can respond.
  • Legal reasons — if required by law, court order or to protect our rights, users or the public.

Within our team, only authorised administrators can see your data, and only to provide the service.

4. Data retention and deletion

  • Your data is kept while your account is active.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account immediately and marks your profile as deleted.
  • Records of requests, payments, refunds, chats and the activity log are kept after account deletion for accounting, refund, dispute-resolution and legal purposes, for as long as those purposes require.
  • To have this remaining data erased as well, email us at the address below. We will delete it unless the law requires us to keep it, and confirm within 30 days.
  • Analytics data is kept according to the Google Analytics for Firebase retention settings and is not linked to your name.

5. Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access is restricted by Firebase security rules so that you can see only your own data, and every administrative action is logged. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

6. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it — most details can be edited in the app (Profile, or a request when we ask for corrections);
  • ask us to delete it;
  • withdraw consent, by deleting your account and stopping use of the app;
  • raise a grievance with us using the contact below.

7. Children

The app is intended for app developers aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date, and significant changes will be announced in the app.
